Paper Planner

A paper-style planner for Windows touchscreen PCs. Write on the pages with your pen, just like a paper day planner.

What it does

  • Year, month, week and day pages that look and feel like a paper planner.
  • Write with a pen or type. Turn pages and zoom with your fingers.
  • Search your own handwriting, using Windows' built-in handwriting recognition.
  • Optionally shows events from your Google Calendar on the pages, and lets you add events to it.
  • Optionally syncs your planner between your own devices through your own OneDrive.

Google Calendar

Connecting Google Calendar is optional. When you connect it, Paper Planner asks Google for permission to:

  • see the list of your calendars, so you can choose which ones appear in the planner, and
  • see, add and change events, so your events show on the right pages and you can add new events from the planner.

Your calendar goes directly between your PC and Google. It is never sent to the developer or anyone else. See the privacy policy.
