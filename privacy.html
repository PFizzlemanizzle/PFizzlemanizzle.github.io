Privacy policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Paper Planner is a Windows app that runs on your own PC. It has no servers and no user accounts of its own, and the developer collects no data about you.

What stays on your PC

Everything you write or type in the planner, and the app's settings, are stored only on your PC, in your Windows user folder (%APPDATA%\Paper Planner). Uninstalling the app does not delete them; you can delete that folder yourself.

Handwriting search uses the handwriting recognition built into Windows, on your PC. Your handwriting is not sent anywhere.

Google Calendar

Connecting Google Calendar is optional. If you connect it, Paper Planner uses Google's sign-in to ask for permission to:

  • View your list of calendars (calendar.calendarlist.readonly), so you can choose which calendars appear in the planner.
  • View and edit events (calendar.events), so your events appear on the planner's pages, and so you can add an event to your calendar when you ask the app to.

How that data is handled:

  • The app talks directly to Google from your PC. Your calendar data is never sent to the developer or to anyone other than Google.
  • Calendar data is used only to show your events in the planner and to create or change events you ask for. Events are kept in memory while the app is open and are not saved to disk. The only calendar details saved, on your PC, are your Google account address and which calendars you chose to show.
  • Your calendar data is never sold, shared, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.
  • To keep you signed in, the app saves a Google sign-in key on your PC, encrypted by Windows so only your Windows account can read it.
  • You can disconnect at any time from the app's menu, or remove its access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Paper Planner's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

OneDrive sync

Syncing is optional. If you turn it on, the app saves a copy of your planner as a file in a Paper Planner folder in your own OneDrive, and Microsoft's OneDrive app uploads it to your OneDrive account. The developer has no access to it.

Children

Paper Planner is not directed at children under 13.
